Qualification

How will qualification work?

Teams will undertake a remote qualification phase in their laboratories, based on the CORSMAL Benchmark protocol. Further qualification requirements will be announced.

When is the qualification deadline?

The timeline and deadlines will be announced on News & Media.

On-site competition

Where will the competition take place?

The track is part of RGMC at ICRA 2027 in Seoul, South Korea. Further on-site details will be announced.

Information about robot availability and requirements will be announced when confirmed.
