Overview

Human-to-Robot Handovers was a track of the 11th Robotic Grasping and Manipulation Competition (RGMC) held at the 2026 IEEE/RAS International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA) in Vienna, Austria.

The track assessed the generalisation capabilities of a robotic control when handing over previously unseen objects filled (or not) with unknown content, hence with a different and unknown mass and stiffness. No object properties are initially known to the robot (or the team) that must infer these properties on-the-fly, during the execution of the dynamic handover, through perception of the scene.
